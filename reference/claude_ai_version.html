Sol (Earth) and the 300 nearest star systems, out to 33.7 light years, placed where they really are. Each system is a territory. Hyperlanes join systems within 7 ly; any system left with fewer than two lanes gets lanes to its nearest neighbours. Continents are real clusters of neighbouring stars, named after the constellation most of their stars lie in. Gold lanes cross between continents.

lane between continentslane inside a continent

Fleets orbit the systems they hold and merge into the biggest ships they can: frigate 1, destroyer 5, battleship 10, dreadnought 25 armies. A ship's size matches its mass, so a dreadnought is as heavy as 25 frigates.

With no game open this is an example deal: systems are shared out at random, each gets 1 ship, then each player places as many again, mostly on border systems.
